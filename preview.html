🌱 Beginner guide — read this and you trade ideas like a professional no math degree needed

Step 0 · What is a trading strategy?

A fixed set of rules: when to buy, when to sell, how much to risk. Example in this repo: "buy when the 10-day average crosses above the 30-day average, sell when it crosses below." No gut feeling, no news-watching — just rules a computer can follow.

Step 1 · What is a backtest?

We pretend-travel to 2015 and let the rules trade old prices. The computer writes down every pretend trade. If it ends richer, the idea might be good. Key trap: the computer can memorize the past (overfitting) — like studying exam answers instead of learning.

Step 2 · What is optimization?

Trying many rule settings (10/30? 5/50? 2/150?) and keeping the best. Helpful — but the "best on old data" is often just the luckiest memorizer. That's why Pardo says: never trust optimization alone.

Step 3 · What is walk-forward? (the hero)

Split history into school (optimize) + exam (test on unseen data), then roll forward and repeat 17 times. Only exam scores count. A strategy that passes most exams is robust. This repo does exactly that — see the sweeping blue/red demo below.

Step 4 · How to read our verdicts

ROBUST = passes ≥50% of exams + efficiency ≥0.5 → worth paper-trading. MARGINAL = mixed → needs filters/risk work. FAIL = fails most exams → don't trade it, no matter how pretty one backtest looked.

Step 5 · What YOU can do next

Change one number in config/experiment.yaml (e.g. OOS window), re-run, watch the verdict change. You now know more than most interview candidates: out-of-sample is the only score that matters.

FAQ (the 6 questions everyone asks)

Does ROBUST mean it will make money next month?

No. It means it survived 17 locked exams. Our own SPY holdout lost −1.4% right after a ROBUST verdict. Trade with stop-loss + monitoring.

Why does FX fail?

Daily MA trend rules need persistent drift. EURUSD 2015–2026 is choppier/mean-reverting at this horizon. Different market type → different verdict. That's the point of multi-market testing.

Why PROM, not just profit?

Net profit crowns the luckiest spike. PROM shrinks wins and inflates losses by √n, penalizing tiny samples. GT-Score/Sharpe are one config flip away for ablation.

Can I add my strategy?

Yes: copy one function in src/pardo_wfa/strategies.py, add its grid to src/pardo_wfa/optimization.py, keep next-open fills + costs. Tests must stay green.

Is this investment advice?

No. Research + education only. Daily, long/flat, Yahoo-grade data. Live capital needs vendor data, intraday path, borrow/latency modelling — all listed as future work.
